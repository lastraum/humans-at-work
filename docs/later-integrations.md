# Later integrations

Not for v1. The floor already has the visit: clock in, walk, talk, take an office.

An integration earns a place here when it is a glance that a human is around. It is off until they turn it on, it shows only while they are on the shift, and it goes away when they clock out. Same family as the city on the door and the project in the window.

It does not play audio, open a second app, or describe their real work in any detail they did not type.

## Now playing

Spotify first. Someone connects their account and, while they are on the clock, their person card can show the track and artist. A small mark on the avatar is enough in the room. The door plate stays name and city.

Read the current track only. Do not embed a player, and do not play it for anyone else. Polling is enough. Stop when the shift ends. Hide it while the office is in Focus, and give them a switch so they can listen without broadcasting.

Last.fm is the wider version later. One connection covers Spotify, Apple Music, and whatever else already scrobbles. Ship Spotify when someone asks. Add Last.fm when the second music app shows up.

## In a meeting

Google Calendar, and only as a yes or no. When a meeting is happening, the door can go Busy on its own. The floor never sees the title, the guests, or the link. They can still set the door by hand, and that choice wins. Clear the automatic Busy when the meeting ends.

This matches the door they already have. It is not a calendar inside the office.

## Links they choose

The card already has a company and one profile link. Later, a few more links they paste: a site, GitHub, X, a booking page. Show the labels they picked. Do not pull a feed, a contribution graph, or a list of repos.

A booking link is the closest thing to "XP for a later collab" until XP has a use. It is still just a link.

## Working on

One short line they type, next to the company. "Writing." "On a draft." "Heads down." The window ad remains the picture they choose. Do not read the editor, the file name, or the repo unless they paste a public name themselves.

## Leave these out

- Sound on the floor, a shared playlist, or a rec-room radio.
- Slack, Discord, or any mirror of another chat.
- A camera, a screen share, or a live view of their desktop.
- Private docs, private repos, commit messages, or document titles.
- Minigames and anything that asks the floor to pay attention.

A later "at the keyboard" heartbeat, from a menu-bar app or an editor, is the same idea as now playing only if it stays a boolean. The moment it names a file, it is out.
